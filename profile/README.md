# US Tech Automations

We build evidence-led automation systems and publish the technical research behind
what qualifies and what does not.

## Data we publish

- [Dated public-record change files](https://ustechautomations.com/feeds/): city
  building permits, energy and water disclosure, federal alcohol permits and other
  United States public records, sealed on dated copies so the changes survive.
  Free samples: [usta-feeds-samples on Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/datasets/gmreincglm/usta-feeds-samples)
- [Machine-readable building-permit fee schedules](https://github.com/USTechAutomations/openpermitfees),
  cited to the document that adopted them.

## Open research

- [Unseen Challenge Foundry](https://github.com/USTechAutomations/unseen-challenge-foundry-os):
  fresh, single-exposure process testing.
- [Cycle Compression Foundry](https://github.com/USTechAutomations/cycle-compression-foundry):
  pre-settlement graph-cycle research.

These research repositories are not claims that a specialised product has qualified.
Each publishes its evidence, tests, exclusions and current decision so buyers can audit
the work before contacting us.
